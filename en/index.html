The space

An oasis inside the sports centre

Manacá is on the ground floor of the new Cuatro Caminos municipal sports centre, at Bravo Murillo 107. Pale wood, dried flowers and warm light: a place to stay a while.

Neighbours come for breakfast, people drop by after training, friends meet for Sunday brunch and remote workers grab a spot at the long tables.

Questions

Before you come

Do I need to book?

No need: just come in. For a big group, send us a WhatsApp and we will tell you the best time.

Do you have gluten-free options?

Yes. Almost all our bakes are gluten-free (made with rice or almond flour) and every sandwich can be made with gluten-free bread.

Are there vegan or vegetarian options?

Yes. The almond cookie is vegan and gluten-free, and there are vegetarian bowls and toasts on the menu.

Where exactly are you?

On the ground floor of the Cuatro Caminos municipal sports centre, Calle de Bravo Murillo 107, between Cuatro Caminos (L1, L2, L6) and Alvarado (L1) metro stations.

Can I work from here?

Yes. There are long tables and plenty of people work here with a coffee.

What are your opening hours?

Monday to Friday 8am–9pm, Saturday 8:30am–9pm and Sunday 9am–9pm.
